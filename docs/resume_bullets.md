# Resume Bullets — Supply Chain Operations Control Tower

_All figures below are pulled directly from `reports/ops_review_2017-09.md`,
generated by `python -m src.run_pipeline` on the full 180,519-row DataCo Smart
Supply Chain dataset. No metric here is invented or estimated._

- Built an end-to-end supply-chain analytics pipeline (SQLite + Python) processing
  180,519 real e-commerce order-item records to compute 6 operational KPIs
  (on-time delivery, SLA adherence, shipping time, cancellation rate, profit
  margin) at monthly/weekly grain, with automated target-breach detection across
  37 monthly periods.

- Engineered a statistical root-cause-analysis engine using segment-level
  contribution decomposition and two-proportion z-tests across 6 operational
  dimensions (region, shipping mode, category, customer segment); surfaced a
  statistically significant late-delivery regression in the "Same Day" shipping
  mode (51.9% → 59.9% late rate) and a Pareto finding that late-delivery failures
  are distributed across 11+ of 15 regions rather than concentrated — reframing
  the issue as systemic rather than localized.

- Implemented an SPC (p-chart) process-stability layer and a transparent,
  rule-based corrective-action recommendation engine, packaged into a 7-tab
  Streamlit dashboard and an auto-generated executive Markdown report, backed by
  a 7-test pytest suite validating the KPI-breach and RCA-contribution logic.
